WORKING DEMONSTRATION / 002

When agent commission grows faster than gross gaming revenue.

An analytical demonstration using synthetic agent-product-month records. The objective is to measure commission affordability, investigate negative-margin accounts and identify records that would require review before approving commission expense.

Python / pandasPostgreSQLSportsbook and virtual productsIllustrative 40% threshold

Business question

High transaction volume does not necessarily mean profitable operations. A commission plan linked to stakes or bet counts may produce costs that are difficult to support when gross gaming revenue is weak.

Method

  • Calculate gross gaming revenue (GGR) as stakes less winnings.
  • Calculate total commission as weekly commission plus monthly commission in a synthetic rule set.
  • Compare aggregate commission cost with aggregate positive GGR only when that denominator is meaningful.
  • Flag rows with non-positive GGR or commission greater than 40% of positive GGR, using 40% as an illustrative analytical review threshold.
Metric definitions:
GGR = stake − winnings.
Commission-to-GGR = total commission ÷ GGR (only if GGR is positive).
Contribution after commission = GGR − total commission.

Watch for denominator traps

Comparing commission with GGR can produce misleading percentages when GGR is zero or negative. This project keeps those cases out of row-level percentage calculations and flags them explicitly instead. A flag indicates review, not wrongdoing.

How to reproduce

Use the synthetic dataset and the project scripts. The browser dashboard uses the same underlying records and does not claim to be a published Power BI report.

python -m pip install -r analysis/requirements.txt
python analysis/commission/analyze.py

Practical decision

Segment commission pressure by product, compare cost exposure with contribution and examine any incentive design that rewards volume without adequate profit protection. Do not treat the sample thresholds as current employer policy.
